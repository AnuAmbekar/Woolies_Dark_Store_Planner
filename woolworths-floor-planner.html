TOGETHERNESS SCORE
How this works: drag a category card into another aisle, or onto "Open new aisle" to give it its own lane. Every product in a category is listed inside its card. Each category also has a keyboard-friendly Move to menu. Empty aisles close automatically.
Togetherness score = every pair of categories sharing an aisle is scored by how strongly their spend correlates (Pearson r, from the correlation matrix). The scores are averaged and rescaled from the −1…+1 range onto 0–100, so 50 is neutral, 100 is perfect co-purchase, 0 is strongly opposed. An aisle with only one category contributes no pairs.
